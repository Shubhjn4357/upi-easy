# Refund & Transaction Dispute Policy — UPI-Easy

**Effective Date:** September 20, 2026  

---

### 1. Independent Non-Custodial Clarification
UPI-Easy is a transaction record management and notification tool. **UPI-Easy does not hold merchant or customer funds and cannot initiate unilateral bank debit reversals, chargebacks, or direct monetary refunds.**

### 2. Handling Failed or Debited Transactions
In UPI transactions, if a customer's bank account has been debited but the merchant has not received credit:
1. **Auto-Reversal (TAT T+1):** Per NPCI and RBI guidelines, interbank UPI transactions that do not settle into the beneficiary account within the stipulated timeframe are automatically reversed back to the customer's bank account by the acquiring/issuing banks (usually within T+1 to T+2 business days).
2. **Bank Grievance / UPI App Dispute:** Customers should raise a transaction dispute directly within their UPI application (e.g. Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) citing the UPI Reference Number (RRN / UTR).

### 3. Merchant Initiated Refunds
If a merchant wishes to issue a refund to a customer for returned merchandise or overpayment:
- The merchant must initiate the refund directly through their linked commercial bank account, net banking portal, or banking business app.
- Merchants can record the refund note in UPI-Easy's ledger for accounting and reconciliation purposes.
